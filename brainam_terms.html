7.Chips, BYOK, fees & refunds

7.1 Chip-based metering

The Services are metered in Chips. One Chip is a unit of AI inference, calculated as:

Chips consumed = Base Tokens × Mode multiplier × Tier multiplier

Mode: Production (1×), Training (2×).
Tier: Basic (1×), Pro (4×), Multi-Modal (6×), Ultra Pro (15×).

Chip consumption is calculated at the time an agent action executes and is final and non-reversible, except where adjusted by our automated reconciliation system (see Section 7.6).

7.2 Chip top-up packs

Brainam offers lifetime, one-time chip top-up packs (currently ₹499, ₹999, ₹1,499 and ₹1,999, subject to change). Paid Chips do not expire so long as your Account remains active and in good standing.

7.3 Bring-Your-Own-Key (BYOK)

BYOK allows you to connect your own AI provider API keys (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google) to your agents. BYOK is offered as either a ₹999 / month recurring subscription or a ₹4,999 lifetime one-time fee, per agent. When BYOK is enabled, you are billed directly by the underlying AI provider for inference, and Brainam does not consume Chips for that agent’s runs.

7.4 Chips are not currency

Chips are not legal tender, currency, prepaid payment instruments, or stored value. They have no cash or monetary value. Chips cannot be redeemed for cash, refunded as cash, transferred, sold, shared or assigned, or used to offset Subscription Fees, BYOK fees, or any other amount owed to Brainam. Chips are solely a unit of measure for usage of the Services.

7.5 No change-of-mind refunds

Because Chips are delivered instantly upon payment and can be consumed immediately, all chip top-up fees and BYOK fees are non-refundable for change of mind or non-use, except as required by law.

Refunds will only be issued in the following circumstances:

  • Duplicate charges resulting from a payment processor error.
  • A successful payment for which Chips or BYOK access were not credited within 48 hours of payment confirmation.
  • Where Brainam terminates your Account without cause under Section 17, in which case unconsumed Chips purchased in the preceding 12 months will be refunded on a pro-rata basis.

Refunds will be issued through the original payment method via our payment processor, Razorpay Software Private Limited, within 7–10 business days of approval.
