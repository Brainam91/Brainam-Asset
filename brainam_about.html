About Brainam

We’re building the AI workforce every business deserves.

Brainam puts a 24/7 AI team inside any business — trained in plain English, priced like a utility, owned by you.

The gap that shouldn’t exist

In India, hiring even one customer-support agent costs upwards of ₹35,000 a month. Add another for nights and weekends and you’re past a lakh before you’ve made your first sale. The bigger you grow, the more this scales — linearly, painfully.

Meanwhile, chatbots are everywhere and almost all of them are terrible. The good AI tools exist, but they’re built for software engineers. You have to learn flowchart software, wire up webhooks, write prompt templates, and pray.

Why we built Brainam

We started Brainam because the gap shouldn’t exist. A founder running a hotel in Goa or a clothing label in Surat shouldn’t need to hire engineers to put a great AI agent on WhatsApp. They should be able to open a screen, talk to the agent like it’s a new hire, correct it when it’s wrong, and ship it the same day.

That’s exactly what the Training Room is. It’s the product. Every Brainam agent is trained by conversation, not by code — and graduates through clear stages (draft → training → mock-tested → live-tested → recruited) so you always know what’s ready and what isn’t.

How we think about pricing

A SaaS subscription that bills you the same whether the agent did 10 messages or 10,000 is a tax on small businesses. So we don’t do that.

You buy chips — the unit of work an agent does. One chip equals one token. Top-ups start at ₹499 and the chips never expire. If you’d rather use your own OpenAI / Anthropic / Gemini API keys, BYOK is one toggle away and skips chip charges entirely for that agent. The agent stays yours either way.

What we believe

Six principles that shape every decision we make about the product.

01

Train like you talk

No flowcharts. No prompt engineering. You teach an agent the way you’d brief a new employee — in your own words, with examples when you have them, with corrections when it gets things wrong.

02

Pay for results, not seats

Chips track the actual work an agent does. Idle agents cost zero. Top-ups have lifetime validity — what you buy is what you keep, no monthly resets, no expiry.

03

Multilingual by default

Built for India and the world. Hindi, Tamil, Marathi, Bengali, Telugu, Punjabi, Gujarati, code-mixed Hinglish — plus 100+ other languages out of the box. No extra setup, no extra cost.

04

Privacy as a feature

DPDP-grade PII redaction is built into every conversation. Every chat is logged in an audit trail you can export anytime. Your data never leaves a provider you didn’t approve.

05

You own your stack

Bring your own keys whenever you want. Export your agent’s training. Cancel anytime. No long-term contracts, no lock-in, no surprise migration bills.

06

Humans where humans matter

The agent escalates to a human the moment a conversation needs one. We’re not building “AI that replaces people” — we’re building AI that handles the 80% of repetitive volume so your team can focus on the 20% that actually needs them.
